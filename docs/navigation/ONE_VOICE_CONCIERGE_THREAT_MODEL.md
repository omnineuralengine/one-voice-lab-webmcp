# ONE Voice Concierge threat model

Scope: the Voice Concierge deterministic, navigation-only ONE Voice Concierge with optional
browser/operating-system speech recognition.

## Assets

- authenticated/guest principal and identity epoch;
- internal route availability and destination authorization boundaries;
- tab-local goal text, speech transcript, interpreted intent, and recommendation
  state;
- provider credentials, budgets, action authority, quotas, and trust tiers
  elsewhere in ONE;
- the access architecture ownership and cross-user isolation invariants;
- the adaptive interface adaptive preference integrity;
- the Scenario Studio Scenario receipt, evidence, and execution integrity;
- accurate provider provenance and limitation disclosures.

## Trust boundaries

1. **Human to browser UI:** typed and spoken text is hostile input. A gesture may
   start one bounded capture, but speech output is never trusted until reviewed.
2. **Browser/OS speech service to ONE:** speech events may be partial, repeated,
   late, oversized, malformed, or generated by a browser-controlled remote
   service. ONE accepts only bounded final text into review.
3. **Resolver to route registry:** the pure resolver emits registered IDs only.
   It cannot emit a route, URL, action, provider, fixture, owner, or grant.
4. **Recommendation to navigation:** a person must explicitly choose a
   destination; the client performs a fresh code-owned lookup and internal
   navigation only.
5. **Navigation to destination:** destination route guards, authentication,
   ownership, quota, confirmation, provider policy, and action authorization are
   independently authoritative.
6. **Tab memory to identity/page/PWA lifecycle:** principal, route, visibility,
   pagehide, BFCache, offline, and unmount events invalidate state; nothing is
   persisted or replayed.

## Blocking security invariants

1. The concierge can navigate only after explicit selection of a registered
   destination.
2. A destination ID, route preview, or presentation depth is not authority.
3. Direct routes enforce their own access and action policies.
4. The browser cannot select a provider, model, action, fixture, owner, trust
   tier, quota, budget, or execution policy through concierge input.
5. Ambiguous, unsupported, malformed, stale, offline-unavailable, and partial
   speech results cannot navigate or execute.
6. Text and confirmed voice input use the same deterministic resolver.
7. Speech recognition starts only after an explicit foreground gesture and one
   active flow is allowed.
8. Cancellation, principal change, navigation, pagehide, BFCache restore, and
   capture-time visibility loss invalidate late results.
9. Concierge artifacts remain tab-local and ephemeral; no new persistence,
   migration, storage key, analytics, export, or sharing surface exists.
10. USER_A state cannot appear for USER_B, and reconnect cannot revive a prior
    principal's work.
11. Provider neutrality preserves provenance and material limitations without
    selecting or ranking a provider.
12. No provider call, credit consumption, upload, save, download, Scenario run,
    or other consequential action occurs inside the concierge.
13. the access architecture ownership/RLS and the Scenario Studio receipt/evidence boundaries remain
    unchanged.
14. Browser/OS speech processing is disclosed truthfully and is not described
    as local, private, offline, or provider-free without external proof.

## Residual risk

Level 1-2 evidence cannot prove browser/OS speech vendor processing, real
microphone and permission behavior, real devices or virtual keyboards, manual
assistive-technology interoperability, installed-PWA lifecycle, deployed
identity propagation, CDN/service-worker/CSP behavior, production logs, or WAF
and abuse controls. These are explicit Level 3 gates. They do not authorize a
server speech path, provider adapter, persistent concierge, or production
activation.
